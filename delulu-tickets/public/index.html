How it works

No screenshots of payments. No paper. Just your QR.

Pick & pay

Choose your passes and pay securely with UPI, cards or netbanking.

Get your QR passes

Instantly on screen and in your email: one QR for each person in your group.

Scan & dance

Show the QR at the gate. Each QR works once, so keep it to yourself.

I'm booking for my friends. How do they get in?

You get one QR per person. Use “Send this pass” on each one to forward it to that friend on WhatsApp, or show them all from your phone and enter together.

Who can buy a Student Pass?

Anyone with a valid college ID card. It is checked at the entrance. Not a student? Pick the General Adult Pass.

Money got deducted but I didn't get my pass?

Don't pay again. Your booking confirms automatically within a few minutes; open “My passes” at the top of this page on the same phone, or contact us with your booking ID.

Can I use a screenshot of my QR?

Yes. A screenshot or printout works. But anyone with that screenshot can use it, and only the first scan gets in.
