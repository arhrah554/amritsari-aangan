## Intent

A cinematic, movie-trailer launch video for the CoachX app: a platform for PTs
and their athletes. The user's words: "super attention grabbing cool
transitions ... like a movie like launch video ... look amazing like we're
revolutionizing the product." Under one minute.

Story follows the deck's own spine: the problem ("Your program is buried in a
chat"), the reveal ("One app. Both sides of the rep."), proof via features (live
program delivery, two-way progress tracking, built for PTs and athletes, the
math: 00 spreadsheets / 01 dashboard / 24/7 accountability), and the close
("Stop guessing. Start training." → "Train smarter. Push harder." → logo).
